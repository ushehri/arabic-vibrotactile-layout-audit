# exploratory

Early checks: `pilot.py` compares acoustic letter distances with sleeve distances on the single-speaker Halabi corpus, and the `l2ksu_*.py` scripts count target-letter confusions in the L2-KSU annotations and TextGrids.
They support no section, table or figure of the paper and produced no reported numbers; the learner and acoustic analyses were rebuilt in `s5.4`/`s5.4-rev` and `full-5.2`/`full-5.2-v2`.
They depend only on the Halabi corpus (`$LMP_DATA/pilot/data/arabic-speech-corpus`) or the L2-KSU corpus (`$LMP_DATA/l2ksu`).
